Who we are

We’re just a few people making the kind of stuff we actually want to play. Most games these days feel like they’re following a checklist. We’re not interested in that.

At Reversal Studios, we start with the ideas that shouldn't work... the ones that feel a bit off, the ones that are hard to label.

We’re a small team by choice. It lets us stay weird and ignore the rules of game design. We aren't here to give you another polished clone of a game you’ve already played. We’re here to reverse your expectations.
